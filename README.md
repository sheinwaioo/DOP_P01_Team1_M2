# DOP\_P01\_Team1\_M2



The waterfall roared like it had secrets to spill. Lila stood at the edge, mesmerised as sunlight fractured into a thousand tiny rainbows in the mist. Every day she came here, she felt the same pull—a whisper in the spray that seemed almost alive.



One afternoon, she noticed a narrow path behind the curtain of water. Heart racing, she stepped through the cascade and found a hidden pool, crystal clear, glowing faintly. She leaned in, and for a heartbeat, the water reflected not her face, but a memory she didn’t know she had—a laughter, a voice, a promise she’d forgotten.



From that day, the waterfall wasn’t just a waterfall. It was a doorway. And Lila knew she would return, chasing the echoes that only it could hold. -Khaleel
